# Terms of Studio Hire

These terms apply to every studio booking with Vision Studios ("we", "us") in London, Dublin, Paris and Istanbul. A booking confirmed by us in writing forms a contract on these terms.

## Bookings and quotes

Quotes are valid for 14 days and are based on the dates, hours, crew and equipment described in the enquiry. A booking is confirmed when we send written confirmation. Changes to dates, hours or requirements after confirmation may change the price and are subject to availability.

## Payment

Unless agreed otherwise in writing, a deposit is payable on confirmation and the balance before the first day of hire. Prices exclude VAT or local sales tax, which is added at the rate in force. We may charge for hours beyond the booked time, for additional crew or equipment requested on the day, and for consumables.

## Cancellation

Cancellations must be made in writing. Cancellation more than 14 days before the booking: deposit retained. Between 14 and 3 days: 50% of the booking fee. Less than 3 days, or no-show: 100% of the booking fee. If we have to cancel for reasons within our control, we refund all sums paid and have no further liability.

The client is responsible for its cast, crew and guests while on our premises and must follow our safety, fire and security instructions. Sets, props and equipment brought in by the client must be removed at the end of the hire. Damage to the studio, fixtures or equipment caused by the client or its party is charged at cost of repair or replacement. Smoking, open flames, pyrotechnics, animals and hazardous materials require our prior written consent.

Equipment listed for each studio is provided in working order and operated by, or under the supervision of, our technical crew. Substitutions of equal specification may be made where necessary. We are not responsible for the performance of equipment brought in by the client.
